New funny questions

Written blind by Fable and Opus (28 ideas). Below: my 10 picks + a funny tiebreaker.

Both = both models came up with the same idea on their own. Those are the safest bets.

1. Someone pulls out a phone for a group selfie. What does Sybil do? Both

  • Takes the phone: "Give, I will take it, your angle is wrong"
  • Hides behind the tallest cousin
  • Film-star pose, perfect smile, first try
  • "Wait wait, one more — my eyes were closed"

2. At a family wedding, where do you find Sybil? Both

  • First on the dance floor, last to leave it
  • Near the food counter, "just checking the quality"
  • Running the whole show — telling the photographer where to stand
  • In a corner with four aunties, solving a family mystery

3. Sybil is in a car. Who is really driving? Both

  • Whoever holds the wheel — she is asleep in 2 minutes
  • The driver, but Sybil from the back seat: "slow, slow, left, LEFT"
  • Sybil herself; nobody else is allowed
  • Google Maps, and Sybil argues with it

4. Sybil's famous dish — the one everyone asks for at every gathering. Both

Typed answer (one dish name)

5. What does Sybil say most often? Opus

  • "Have you eaten?"
  • "Wear your glasses properly!"
  • "I told you so."
  • "Just five minutes more."

6. Somebody in the family sneezes. Sybil's instant advice: Fable

  • "Drink hot water"
  • "Don't rub your eyes!"
  • "Come to the clinic tomorrow, 9 sharp"
  • "This is from sitting under the fan"

7. If Sybil got one superpower tomorrow, she would pick: Fable

  • X-ray vision (obviously)
  • Being in Bidar and Bangalore at the same time
  • Never needing sleep
  • Making every relative answer the phone on the first ring

8. Power cut in the middle of dinner. Sybil: Fable

  • Lights a candle and carries on as if nothing happened
  • Rings the electricity office personally, by name
  • Announces "Good, now everyone will talk"
  • Switches on the phone torch and checks everyone's eyes

9. Which "doctor habit" does Sybil bring home without noticing? Opus

  • Checks everyone's eyes at the dinner table
  • Asks "Can you read this line?" about the TV news ticker
  • Says "Look up... look down..." during family photos
  • Writes the grocery list so nobody else can read it

10. It is 5:30 am. Where is Sybil? Fable

  • Fast asleep, phone on silent
  • Already dressed, already on the phone to the hospital
  • Walking laps of the compound like it is a race
  • Watering the plants and giving them instructions

TIEBREAKER (replaces surgeries): How many pairs of spectacles does the eye doctor herself own — counting the ones "somewhere in the car"? Fable

Closest number wins. Funnier than surgeries, and only Sybil knows.
